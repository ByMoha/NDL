# Moments and Cases

Tone follows the user's situation. Each **moment** has a moment tone. Each **case** within it
takes a **different** tone that leads the microcopy, plus a mode: faith-led or logistical.

```
① Baseline     the five qualities — always on
② Moment tone  set by the situation
③ Case tone    a DIFFERENT tone — leads the copy
④ Mode         faith-led OR logistical
```

If a screen stacks several blocks, each block gets its own moment assignment.

| # | Moment | Description | Moment tone |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | Welcome & Entry | The user arrives at a destination in the app | Hospitable & Serving |
| 02 | Tasks & Input | The user performs a task such as entering data | Guiding & Knowledgeable |
| 03 | Decision Points | The user must decide how to move forward | Guiding & Knowledgeable |
| 04 | Errors & Recovery | An error occurred; the user needs a way to resolve it | Empathetic & Caring |
| 05 | Blocks & Restrictions | A gate stops the user finishing a task | Guiding & Knowledgeable |
| 06 | Faith Guidance | The app guides the user through worship or ritual | Wise & Accurate |
| 07 | Success & Completion | The user finished a task | Guiding & Knowledgeable |
| 08 | Consolation | The system cancelled or rescheduled a sacred experience | Empathetic & Caring |
| 09 | Alerts & Warnings | Mass alerts and warnings in emergencies | Guiding & Knowledgeable |

## 01 · Welcome & Entry — Hospitable & Serving

### Case 1 · First time / onboarding
**Case tone:** Guiding & Knowledgeable · **Mode:** Logistical

| | |
|---|---|
| ✔ | "Welcome to the Nusuk Marketplace. Your place to make all your bookings for your Umrah or Hajj journeys." |
| ✘ | "Welcome. You're in the Nusuk Marketplace." — abrupt, too little context |
| AR | `مرحبًا بك في سوق نسك. مكانك لإتمام حجوزات رحلات العمرة أو الحج.` |

Welcomes, orients, and names what they can do. Courteous host stance.

### Case 2 · Routine visit
**Case tone:** Guiding & Knowledgeable · **Mode:** Logistical

| | |
|---|---|
| ✔ | "Welcome back to the Nusuk Marketplace." |
| ✘ | "Welcome again." — abrupt, no place cue |
| AR | `مرحبًا بعودتك إلى سوق نسك.` |

### Case 3 · Sacred welcome
**Case tone:** Wise & Accurate · **Mode:** Faith-led

| | |
|---|---|
| ✔ | "Welcome to the blessed Rawdah of The Prophet ﷺ." |
| ✘ | "Welcome to the Prophet's Rawdah." — misses protocol and the sense of occasion |
| AR | `مرحبًا بك في الروضة المباركة لرسول الله ﷺ.` |

## 03 · Decision Points — Guiding & Knowledgeable

### Case 1 · Consent to proceed
**Case tone:** Hospitable & Serving · **Mode:** Logistical

| | |
|---|---|
| ✔ | "By clicking 'PAY', you confirm all the details you have filled in are correct.<br>Please click 'PAY' when you are ready to proceed." |
| ✘ | "Click 'PAY' when you're ready." — incomplete; skips what the click confirms |
| AR | `بالضغط على «ادفع»، تؤكّد أن جميع البيانات التي أدخلتها صحيحة.` |

### Case 2 · Destructive / irreversible
**Case tone:** Empathetic & Caring · **Mode:** Logistical

| | |
|---|---|
| ✔ | "Are you sure you'd like to proceed? You are about to cancel your hotel booking.<br>Once you cancel your hotel booking, your booking details will no longer be available." |
| ✘ | "Are you sure you want to proceed?" — vague, no consequence |
| AR | `هل تريد المتابعة؟ أنت على وشك إلغاء حجز الفندق.` |

Names the act and the consequence. Cuts surprise later.

## 04 · Errors & Recovery — Empathetic & Caring

### Case 1 · Fixable error
**Case tone:** Guiding & Knowledgeable · **Mode:** Logistical

| | |
|---|---|
| ✔ | "That's unfortunate.<br>Your payment did not go through.<br>Please check the number you entered and try again." |
| ✘ | "Payment did not go through. Check the number you have entered and try again." — accurate, but cold when people are already disappointed |
| AR | `هذا مؤسف.`<br>`لم يكتمل الدفع.` |

Empathy first, then the fix.

### Case 2 · System failure / offline
**Case tone:** Guiding & Knowledgeable · **Mode:** Logistical

| | |
|---|---|
| ✔ | "You may not be connected to the internet.<br>Please check your connection and try again." |
| ✘ | "There is no internet connection. Check your connection and try again." — blunt diagnosis, thin on courtesy |
| AR | `قد لا تكون متصلًا بالإنترنت.`<br>`يرجى التحقق من اتصالك والمحاولة مرة أخرى.` |

### Case 3 · Anxious wait
**Case tone:** Hospitable & Serving · **Mode:** Logistical

| | |
|---|---|
| ✔ | "The visa for your Umrah visit is in progress.<br>We will notify you as soon as possible when it is complete, inshaAllah." |
| ✘ | "Pending! Check later." — no context, invites frustration |
| AR | `تأشيرة زيارة العمرة قيد المعالجة.`<br>`سنُعلمك في أقرب وقت عند اكتمالها، إن شاء الله.` |

Status plus next signal. Steady under wait.

## 05 · Blocks & Restrictions — Guiding & Knowledgeable

Every case here opens with the same bridge — "You're almost there" / `أنت على وشك الإتمام` —
because a block is the moment a user is most likely to abandon. The bridge is what keeps
frustration down; don't strip it for brevity.

### Case 1 · Correctable block
**Case tone:** Empathetic & Caring · **Mode:** Logistical

| | |
|---|---|
| ✔ | "You're almost there.<br>Add one more person to your party to complete the booking information." |
| ✘ | "Add another person to continue." — misses the bridge |
| AR | `أنت على وشك الإتمام.` |

### Case 2 · Hard eligibility
**Case tone:** Empathetic & Caring · **Mode:** Logistical

| | |
|---|---|
| ✔ | "You're almost there.<br>A confirmation code has been sent to you by email. Please enter it below to continue." |
| ✘ | "We have sent you a confirmation code by email." — incomplete, no next step |
| AR | `أنت على وشك الإتمام.` |

### Case 3 · Time-limited hold
**Case tone:** Hospitable & Serving · **Mode:** Logistical

| | |
|---|---|
| ✔ | "You're almost there.<br>Complete the payment in the next 10 minutes to proceed." |
| ✘ | "You have 10 minutes to pay before the order is cancelled." — informs, skips host manners |
| AR | `أنت على وشك الإتمام.` |

Note the difference in the ✘: it frames the deadline as a threat. The ✔ frames the same fact as
a path forward. Urgency with courtesy.

## 06 · Faith Guidance — Wise & Accurate

### Case 1 · Step guidance
**Case tone:** Guiding & Knowledgeable · **Mode:** Faith-led

| | |
|---|---|
| ✔ | "Tawaf is a rukn of Umrah. Begin at the Black Stone line and keep the Kaaba on your left." |
| ✘ | "You MUST start correctly or your Umrah is INVALID." |

**Mustahab enrichment:** "For extra reward, you can recite dua as you walk." — invite, don't
instruct.

**Ikhtilaf made practical:** "You can pray your Nafl salah anywhere in the Haram after Tawaf."
Not: "You must pray behind Maqam Ibrahim."

See `faith-content-rules.md` for the full phrasing patterns by ruling type.

### Case 2 · Silent accompaniment
**Case tone:** Empathetic & Caring · **Mode:** Faith-led

| | |
|---|---|
| ✔ | "Circuit 3 of 7" |
| ✘ | "Amazing job!! Keep crushing your Tawaf!!" |

The app recedes. During worship, minimal and reverent beats encouraging.

## 07 · Success & Completion — Guiding & Knowledgeable

### Case 1 · Sacred completion · Blessing
**Case tone:** Hospitable & Serving · **Mode:** Faith-led

| | |
|---|---|
| ✔ | "AlhamduLillah, by the grace of Allah (SWT), you have completed the booking for your visit to The Rawdah of The Prophet ﷺ.<br>Please see the permit confirmation attached." |
| ✘ | "The booking for the Rawdah has been completed." — no blessing lead, no path to the permit, thin courtesy |
| AR | `الحمد لله، بتيسير من الله، أكملت حجز زيارتك إلى روضة رسول الله ﷺ.` |

Blessing first, then the practical next look.

### Case 2 · Logistical completion · Receipt
**Case tone:** Hospitable & Serving · **Mode:** Logistical

| | |
|---|---|
| ✔ | "You have completed your hotel booking and your payment has been processed.<br>Please see the attached receipt for your booking." |
| ✘ | "The booking for the Hotel has been completed." — no path to the receipt, thin courtesy |
| AR | `أكملت حجز الفندق وعُولج الدفع.` |

**Logistical mode: no sacred blessing lead.** This is the clearest place the mode switch changes
the copy — same moment, same case tone, different opening.

Note the Arabic in both cases uses **أكملت** — the user as actor, addressed directly — and
`عُولج` for the system action (light passive). Never `تم`.

## 08 · Consolation — Empathetic & Caring

### Case 1 · A sacred plan did not happen (cancelled or missed rite)
**Case tone:** Guiding & Knowledgeable · **Mode:** Logistical

| | |
|---|---|
| ✔ | "We regret to inform you that your visit to The Rawdah has been cancelled due to unplanned maintenance work.<br>Please accept our apologies.<br>Please try to book a slot for your next Rawdah visit here. (link)" |
| ✘ | "Your booking for your visit has been cancelled." — fact only; at a low moment that adds friction |
| AR | `نأسف لإبلاغك بأن زيارتك إلى الروضة أُلغيت بسبب أعمال صيانة غير مخططة.` |

Regret, apology, then a way forward. Softens disappointment without hiding the fact. A
consolation with fact only and no next step should be escalated for a second pass.

## 09 · Alerts & Warnings — Guiding & Knowledgeable

### Case 1 · Heads-up
**Case tone:** Empathetic & Caring · **Mode:** Logistical

| | |
|---|---|
| ✔ | "Please note: The time you are planning for your Umrah rituals, the Tawaf area will be busy and crowded. Please prioritise your health when planning your Umrah rituals." |
| ✘ | "Tawaf will be crowded at the time you're planning to do Umrah." — fact without care or advice |

Names the risk and puts health first.

### Case 2 · Imperative
**Case tone:** Hospitable & Serving · **Mode:** Logistical

| | |
|---|---|
| ✔ | "Please exit The Rawdah area as soon as possible.<br>Thank you for your kind cooperation." |
| ✘ | "Exit the Rawdah area." — adequate command, thin manners for a sensitive place |
| AR | `يرجى مغادرة منطقة الروضة في أقرب وقت ممكن.`<br>`شكرًا لتعاونك الكريم.` |

## Moments beyond the product

Long-form isn't triggered by a user action in a flow, so it has no case — but it still takes a
moment tone and a mode, and the baseline qualities still apply.

| Surface | Moment tone | Mode |
|---|---|---|
| Social — educational (Zad, Explore) | Wise & Accurate | Faith-led |
| Social — engagement | Hospitable & Serving | Logistical |
| Campaign and landing copy | Guiding & Knowledgeable | Logistical |
| Release notes | Guiding & Knowledgeable | Logistical |
| Help articles and FAQs | Guiding & Knowledgeable | Logistical |
| Support macros and app store replies | Empathetic & Caring | Logistical |
| Religious and educational articles | Wise & Accurate | Faith-led |

Structures and approved examples are in `content-types.md`.
