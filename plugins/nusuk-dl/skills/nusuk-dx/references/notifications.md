# Notifications

Notifications reach the user in a direct moment and shape their decisions. They are never random
and never purely promotional — each one is an extension of the Companion's voice.

---

## Before writing any notification

**1. Does this deserve a notification?** Only if it carries a direct call to interact — book,
activate, remind — or time-bound information: a blessed day, a worship time. If it's neither,
it doesn't ship.

**2. Define one goal.** Every notification carries exactly one clear goal.

**3. The title carries the goal.** Clear, specific, containing the action or the core idea.
Under six words.

**4. The body adds, never repeats.** It clarifies the benefit or supports with new information.
A body that mirrors the title wastes the only two lines you get.

---

## Anatomy

| Part | Rule |
|---|---|
| Title | Under 6 words. Carries the goal. |
| Body | Adds context and the next step. |
| Together | 10–15 words total. |
| Update notifications | Short sentences, max ~140 characters. |

---

## Reminder and engagement notifications

- **The title shows the goal, it doesn't tease it.**
  ✔ "Performing Umrah erases sins and raises your rank"
  ✘ "Do you know the virtue of Umrah?"
- **Link faith value to a practical step** — a virtue paired with a service the user can act on
  inside the app.
- **Complement, don't repeat**, between title and body.
- **Every notification offers an invitation or a benefit.** No purely descriptive or celebratory
  sends.

---

## Update notifications

- **Clarity** — deliver the information simply and fast.
- **Brevity** — short sentences, max ~140 characters.
- **User-centred, not system-centred.**
  ✔ "We received your report. Rest assured, we'll follow up, by Allah's will."
  ✘ "Report submission has been processed."
- **No repetition** between title and body; each part adds value.
- Keep the companionable tone, with blessing phrases in their right places.

---

## Scripture in notifications

Governed by `faith-content-rules.md`, with three notification-specific rules:

- **Context-bound.** The ayah or hadith relates directly to the notification's topic — never
  linguistic decoration.
- **One text only.** Never more than one ayah or hadith per notification.
- **Placement follows role.** If the sacred text is the core of the message, lead with it and
  follow with light guidance. If it supports, close with it.

---

## Mode and tone

A notification takes its moment tone from what it's about, and its mode from whether it's
faith-led or logistical — the same as any other block. See `moments-and-cases.md`.

- A departure reminder is **logistical**, Guiding & Knowledgeable.
- A rite-completion congratulation is **faith-led**, and carries a blessing.
- A cancelled Rawdah visit is **Consolation** — regret, apology, and a way forward, never a bare
  fact.

**No emoji on faith-led notifications**, errors, or consolations. See the emoji rules in
`english-style.md`.

---

## Mechanics

- **Name the subject.** A user with three bookings can't act on an ambiguous reminder.
- **Deep-link to the screen that resolves it.** A notification landing on the home screen wastes
  the tap.
- **Time-relative for imminent events** ("in 30 minutes"), absolute beyond a day. Never make the
  user compute.
- **One notification per event.** Batch anything that would fire twice.
- **Front-load the essential fact** so it survives lock-screen truncation.
- Arabic runs longer than English — budget from the Arabic. See `localization.md`.
