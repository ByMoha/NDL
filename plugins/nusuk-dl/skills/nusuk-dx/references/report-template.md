# Report Template

The team's structure, ready to fill. Keep the headings — reports are compared across evaluations,
and a renamed section breaks that.

---

## Summary

Two to four sentences. What was evaluated, the headline finding, and the single most important
action. Someone who reads only this paragraph should know whether to worry.

---

## 1. Usability strengths

> Identify these **first**. It is essential to name what already works so it doesn't get lost in
> a redesign.

| Strength | Reasoning |
|---|---|
| What works | Why it works — tie it to a heuristic or principle where you can |

---

## 2. Usability problems

| UX problem | Location in product | Principle violated | Severity | Ease | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| What the problem is | Where it occurs — list every location | Which heuristic or Rams principle | S0–S4 | E0–E4 | The specific change |

Where the same problem appears in several places, keep it as **one row** and list the locations.
Frequency belongs in the grouping step, not as duplicate rows.

---

## 3. Patterns

| Pattern | Evidence | What it implies |
|---|---|---|
| The recurring theme | Which issues above point at it | The structural conclusion |

Run each pattern through the diagnostic router — adoption / follow-through / uncertainty /
distrust / community — and name which one it lands on.

---

## 4. Prioritised actions

Sorted by severity, then by ease within a band. **Lead with the high-severity, low-effort
cluster** — that is the list the team acts on this week.

| # | Change | Outcome | Severity | Ease |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | What to change | What that change leads to | S4 | E1 |

Each change must be concrete enough for a designer to open Figma and act on it. "Provide clearer
error messages that guide the user on how to rectify the mistake", not "improve error messages".

## Notes for the reader

Close with what you could not evaluate and what would need testing to confirm. A heuristic
evaluation is expert judgement — strong priors, not measurements — and saying which findings
would benefit from usability testing is part of the deliverable, not a hedge.
