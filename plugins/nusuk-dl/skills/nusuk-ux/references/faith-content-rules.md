# Faith content and religious warmth

Use religious language in proportion to the actual moment. Worship context does not make every logistical message a religious message.

## Religious warmth

A blessing expresses a wish, not a guarantee. Do not turn it into a factual assurance. Follow the specific moment rather than requiring a blessing in every faith-related string.

Emojis occur in Nusuk’s completion and sharing copy. Preserve contextual precedent rather than automatically stripping or adding them. Do not decorate or alter supplied Quranic/Hadith text.

## Selected revisions

| Context | Earlier wording | Latest Rawdah wording |
|---|---|---|
| Completed worship | تقبل الله زيارتك ودعاءك، ويسّر لك العودة إلى هذا المقام الطاهر | تقبل الله صلاتك في الروضة الشريفة |
| Hope after disruption | نسأل الله أن يأجرك على نيتك ويرزقك زيارةً مباركةً قريبًا | نرجو أن تتيسر لك زيارةً أخرى قريبًا |
| Maintenance cancellation | تم إلغاء الزيارة لأعمال الصيانة / ولك أجر النية بإذن الله | تم إلغاء الزيارة بسبب أعمال الصيانة |

**Reasoning:** make completion blessings relevant to the act; express restrained hope after disappointment; explain operational cancellations without adding reward assurances. These are contextual choices, not a ban on Duaa or a requirement to make every message secular.

Aligned completion pair: **تقبل الله صلاتك في الروضة الشريفة** / **May Allah accept your Salah in Rawdah**.

Availability exception: **ستُتاح مقاعد جديدة للساعة (%s) قريبًا بإذن الله** / **New seats for (%s) will be available soon, inshaAllah.** This shows that a logistical message may retain religious warmth. It does not authorize inventing seat availability or adding a blessing to every payment message.

## Religious guidance

The content team supplies the religious guidance, its classification where relevant, qualifications and intended position. The writer expresses that content; do not derive a ruling from a glossary or an example.

| Supplied classification | Writing approach |
|---|---|
| Rukn | State its supplied status calmly; preserve qualifications. |
| Wajib | State its supplied status clearly. Include a consequence only when provided for this exact case. |
| Mustahab | Make optionality clear with an invitation or the supplied recommendation. Do not invent reward claims or “many pilgrims…” social proof. |
| Ikhtilaf | Follow the supplied position and disclosure requirements. Do not independently select an easier opinion or remove a qualification merely to shorten copy. |

Only ask for classification when writing actually depends on it. Ordinary labels, booking messages and blessings do not all require fiqh classification. Never treat generic descriptions such as “missing a wajib always requires Fidyah” as universal rules.

For an ayah or Hadith, use the exact supplied approved text and attribution. Do not invent a quotation, paraphrase it as if verbatim, or describe an unverified English rendering as an accepted translation. If EN is needed but absent, flag the missing approved rendering. A source example alone does not establish scholarly verification.

Landing is unfinished: its prayer-time examples cannot establish a universal rule requiring Quranic verses in every notification. Use the approved content selection for the requested surface. Preserve source attribution supplied for the task; do not claim a government or scholarly body reviewed text without evidence.
