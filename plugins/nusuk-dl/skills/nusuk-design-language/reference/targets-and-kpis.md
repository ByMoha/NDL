# Targets and KPIs

200M engaged users needs a repeatable engine: value fast, repeated reliably, trust preserved, revenue without harming the mission. Every feature names its pillar, its success threshold and its guardrail before handoff.

## North Star Metric: Weekly Meaningful Actions per Active User (WMA/WAU)

Validated meaningful actions per active user per week. **Behavioural outcomes, not taps:** an on-time prayer check-in, a Quran session over a threshold, a dhikr set, a charity action, family outreach, a learning module, a booking milestone, a permit confirmed, a guidance step, a resolved issue. Every action has a minimum threshold so it can't be gamed.

**Active user:** completes a meaningful action (preferred), starts a core workflow step (booking, payment, help), or views a key detail screen and acts on it (save, share, filter). **Login alone doesn't count.**

**Cohorts:** first meaningful action, channel (organic, paid, partner), region and language, journey type (rituals, daily habits, commerce and services); retention at D1, D7, D30 and after the first value moment.

## A · Adoption — time to value

| Tier | Definition |
|---|---|
| A0 First value moment | One meaningful action in the first session |
| A1 Activation (24h) | Two actions within 24 hours, or one core loop plus a return |
| A2 Commitment (7d) | The same action category twice in 7 days |

Supporting: signup conversion, time to first meaningful action, onboarding completion, drop-offs (OTP failure, permissions, ignored first prompt).

## B · Engagement — habit loop strength

WAU/MAU stickiness · meaningful actions per week per WAU · loop repeat rate (a core action 3+ times a week) · routine stability (consistent time windows) · **return after lapse within 48 hours, the "mercy loop"** · feature adoption diagnostics · feature-to-retention uplift at D7 and D30.

## C · Satisfaction and trust

- **Moment CSAT** at registration complete, first value moment, payment success or failure, reservation confirmed, complaint resolved — after stable outcomes, never during anxiety peaks. Sampling and cooldowns apply.
- **Trust Index:** payment success rate, refund and chargeback rate, complaints per 1,000, 48-hour reopen rate, rating trend weighted by volume, notification opt-out rate.

## D · Revenue and unit economics

GMV, gross and net revenue · funnel: view → hold → payment start → payment success · leakage: payment failures, hold expiry, cancellations · refund, chargeback and dispute rates by reason.

## Operational quality (leading indicators)

Complaints per 1,000 users and per 1,000 transactions · time to first response and to resolution · reopen within 48 hours · friction signals (rage taps, repeated attempts, form error loops) · rating trend and top five negative themes.

## DX team targets

86 CSAT · 4.6 app rating · UX maturity: heuristics at 100%, the 3Ms followed, consistent DL and services, knowledge sharing · 3 awards (international and local) · one knowledge-sharing session per member each month.

## The paired-metric rule

Every success metric ships with a guardrail. A reminder feature tracks completions **and** notification opt-out; a commerce feature tracks conversion **and** refunds and complaints; a habit feature tracks repeat rate **and** return-after-lapse.
