# Brand

Nusuk is the faith companion for the needs of every Muslim. The brand guidelines (Ver 1.0, edition of November 2026, art studio, received 17 November 2026; replaces the edition of 8 November) explain where the DL comes from and how Nusuk should feel; in product screens, the DL tokens apply. The pages are in the design system's assets: *Brand* (foundation to iconography), *Illustration*, *Art* (art direction) and *Applications*.

**What this edition adds:** brand story and personality pages, sound characteristics, logo minimum sizes and placement, the primary and secondary colour specs (RGB and CMYK), colour usage per colour, six colour scenarios (data visualisation among them), the Arabic type hierarchy, a 24-subject illustration library, the AI prompt for new illustrations, and a new chapter, **Art direction**: the 5% margin, the Nusuk, Makkah and Madinah frames, layout rules and the social feed rhythm. Where the guidelines and the product DL meet, see *Where brand and DL meet* at the end.

## Story, values, personality

**Story (*brand-story*).** The Nusuk Faith Experience serves everyday Muslim life and the Hajj and Umrah pilgrimage in one place. Hospitality and service from the host, Saudi Arabia, are part of the pilgrim's success; empathy, because every Muslim and pilgrim has different needs; guidance and knowledge, expressed with wisdom, care and respect for the sacred.

| Value (*brand-values*) | What it means in a screen |
|---|---|
| Hospitality & Service | The warmth and generosity of a good host, rooted in Saudi culture. Welcome, never command. |
| Empathy & Care | Every Muslim and pilgrim has different needs, met with consideration and kindness in the moment. Design for the elderly, first-timers and people under stress. |
| Guidance & Knowledge | Supplied information, presented clearly, and only the guidance supported for the task. |
| Wisdom & Accuracy | The supplied, approved sources for each piece of guidance, communicated with care and sound judgement. Do not infer verification from the brand expression alone. |
| Imaan & The Sacred | Faith-led guidance and rituals treated with respect; madhab-neutral when expressing a supplied position. |

**Persona (*brand-persona*):** the Host, the Companion, the Alim, the Source, the Imaani.

**Personality (*brand-personality*):** Caregiver + Sage, 50 / 50: warmth and empathy with knowledge and wisdom. Five traits: Hospitable & Serving, Empathetic & Caring, Guiding & Knowledgeable, Wise & Accurate, Faith-led & Sacred.

## Verbal standards

Four tones: hospitable and serving, empathetic and caring, guiding and knowledgeable, wise and accurate (*verbal-standards*). The fifth trait becomes a **mode switch**: faith-led (إيماني: an approved blessing after worship, supplied ritual guidance) or logistical (عملي: confirmed actions, status updates, error recovery). Choose one per message; never both in the same block.

| Be (*verbal-arabic*) | Never (*verbal-never*) |
|---|---|
| واضح وودود · Simple words close to the heart | Preachy or scolding · Nusuk informs; the pilgrim acts |
| مهذب ومرحّب · We honour the guest; no harsh directives, no mockery | Harsh or mocking · Courtesy, even under urgency |
| رسمي بقدر يعزز المصداقية · Formal enough for the sanctity, without stiffness | Exaggerating or over-promising · Strengths as they are |
| مطمئن · The user feels things are under control | Madhab-partial · We never pick a side |
| إيماني · Expressions of faith in their natural places | Stiff or affected · Professional, and simple |

## Sound

**Noor Al Wusool** (*brand-sound*, *sound-characteristics*): "because every journey deserves a welcome that feels worthy". It opens in Maqam Hijaz (anticipation: an authentic Arabic character and spiritual depth), carries the emotional memory of Tala' Al-Badru 'Alayna (arrival and welcome), and moves from its usual Maqam Sika into Maqam Ajam (joy: brighter and accessible to a global audience). Sonic personality: warm, welcoming, radiant, human, emotional. Its role: moments of arrival, completion and celebration, and films, campaigns and events. In the product, the sound inventory in the Nusuk project assigns the cues, and nothing autoplays (M10).

## Logo

- **Brandmark and wordmark** form one lockup, in Arabic and English (*logo-lockup*). Use the files only (*Assets › Logos*); never rebuild the mark.
- **Clear space:** 1X on every side; no text, imagery or graphics inside it (*logo-clear-space*).
- **Minimum size (width):** brandmark 25 px digital, 8 mm print; full lockup 70 px digital, 25 mm print.
- **Placement:** in the defined layout areas, consistently, so it lines up across touchpoints (*logo-placement*).
- **Colours (*logo-colours*):** Primary Lockup Black is the default, on light or neutral backgrounds. Primary Lockup White, on dark backgrounds and imagery. Secondary Lockup Gold is reserved for selected branded applications where a more elevated expression fits. The page also shows the brandmark alone in rounded tiles (gold, black, light), the app-icon form.
- **Never (*logo-misuse*):** stretch, rotate, recolour, make unofficial lockups, shrink parts of the logo, outline it, add drop shadows, add gradients, or place it inside a box.

## Nusuk Companion

Nusuk personified. Not a mascot beside the platform and not an assistant above it: it is present as identity everywhere and speaks only where continuity would otherwise break (*companion-position*, *companion-behaviour*, *companion-hats*, *companion-shape*).

| It refuses to be | Why |
|---|---|
| A chat assistant | Chat is a destination; destinations compete with services for entry, and win |
| A floating bubble or FAB | A persistent overlay becomes a second navigation model every service has to compete with |
| A mascot | Decoration with no mandate becomes noise, gets cut, and takes the brand's warmth with it |
| A shell or launcher | Sitting above a service estate is how other assistants failed |
| A spiritual guide | Worship is between the guest and Allah; nothing on the platform stands in that line |

**The loop has three beats and no fourth.** A behaviour that fits none of them belongs to a service, not to the Companion.

| Beat | Does | Does not |
|---|---|---|
| Nudge | Senses a need from time, location or schedule ("Your Rawdah permit slot opens in 2 hours"). States what, when and one action. Fires at a flow boundary | Explain, sell, persuade, add a second message, or fire mid-task |
| Hand off | One tap opens the owning service with the state already filled (Nusuk Maps with the destination). The service takes over in its own voice | Recreate the map, the booking or the form inside a bubble; narrate the handoff |
| Step back | Inside the service it is identity only. For a stuck guest, support is the door; the Companion routes and goes quiet | Resolve, answer or hover |

**Hats set the tone, not the reach.**

| Hat | Standing presence | Nudge | Live activity | Blocker | Constraint |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Logistical: flights, permits, transport, wayfinding, appointments | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes | — |
| Spiritual: prayer, athkar, Quran, rites | Present only | No | No | No | Protected channel. No initiation, no confirmation requests, no praise, no tally. Rulings quoted and attributed, never adjudicated. Madhab-neutral |
| Transactional: booking, payment, marketplace | Collision only | Yes | Yes | Yes | The service voice leads; the Companion appears only where two services collide |
| Institutional: rejections, failures, declines | Present only | No | No | No | An accountable "we". No face on a rejection, no consolation that reads as dismissive |
| Marketing: campaigns, offers, discovery | No | No | No | No | Fullest on social and owned channels; no in-app reach at any tier. One campaign in-app makes every nudge it ever sent suspect |

Two forms: Makkah (gold) and Madinah (green), with a small set of expressions. The components live in the *Nusuk Companion* file. In the DL, the Nudge above the bottom navigation is the Companion's nudge: one line, one action, never a promotion.

## Imagery

Calm, clear and authentic. Four types: journey and experience, sacred landmarks, spiritual atmosphere, architecture (*imagery-direction*, *imagery-journey*, *imagery-landmarks*, *imagery-atmosphere*, *imagery-architecture*). Soft light, spacious compositions, stillness. Selections stay connected to Makkah, Madinah and the sacred destinations of the journey; the pages show the look, not prescribed places or moments. Avoid staged worship close-ups and anything that turns worship into a performance.

- **Selective focus** (*imagery-treatment-focus*): pick an image with a clear subject, define the focal point, then blur away from it gradually; leave some surrounding detail in focus.
- **Soft grain** (*imagery-treatment-grain*): one of four grain textures over the photo, Soft Light blending, 50% opacity at most. The subject stays clear and the original tones recognisable.

**A new illustration** (*illustration-creating*) is made with the Nusuk visual system skill. The prompt has three lines and a closing instruction:

```
$nusuk-visual-system
Create: [subject, action and location]
Format: [aspect ratio / intended use]
Must include: [optional essential details]
Lead the creative and art direction, generate the visual, then QA and refine it against our approved references.
```

Example: *Create:* the Makkah Clock Tower overlooking the city. *Format:* 16:9 landscape thumbnail. *Must include:* a clear crescent and clock face, Gold + Deep Blue, open space on the right for a headline. Review the result and ask for targeted adjustments. The guidelines record that the system was developed with GPT-6.1 Sol. The skill keeps background people as soft, pale suggestions and a focal person or group coherent. Portraits of scholars and reciters are licensed content; never generate one (DL-10).

## Nusuk visual system (14 November 2026)

The full account skill `nusuk-visual-system`, included in the Nusuk DL on 14 November 2026 so the illustration rules travel with the DL. It governs watercolour illustrations; product UI stays with the DL tokens and components.

**Files:** `visual-system/approved-set.md` (the 17 selected artworks and the scene guide), `visual-system/colour.md` (colour matching), `visual-system/manifest.json` (exact files and hashes) and `visual-system/images/` (17 artworks and the colour reference, unaltered originals). The images ship only in `nusuk-design-language-with-artworks.zip`; the small zip embedded in the DL guide leaves them out. If `visual-system/images/` is missing in your copy, say that the reference set was not inspected. The helper `scripts/match_blue_tone.py` named in `colour.md` was not supplied with the references and is not in this skill.

### Reference standard

Use the selected artworks and scene guide. **Makkah City (SEL-09)** defines the general painting language and default blue. The closest selected scene defines its own composition and construction. Use a supplied colour reference when explicitly requested; **Asset 39 (COL-01)** is bundled for the selected Umrah Journey palette. Exact files are recorded in the reference manifest.

Inspect the actual images before creating or editing. Transfer their painting language without importing unrelated subjects. Preserve selected originals unchanged and keep new versions separate. Later explicit instructions take precedence.

### Painting language

- **One focal subject or connected passage.** Broad joined deep-blue shadows often approach a silhouette. Sparse, soft gold planes reveal volume. Maintain convincing anatomy and identifying geometry.
- **Minimal construction.** Build forms with a few purposeful watercolor planes and reserved paper. Reduce modeled muscles, repeated folds and tiny mechanisms. Minimality comes from economical brushwork, not merely removing objects, fading a detailed render or applying texture.
- **Selective sharpness.** Keep identifying focal contours confident. Let peripheral cloth, wool or architecture merge into compatible atmosphere or shadow. Avoid outlines, uniform blur and all-over sharpening.
- **Quiet support.** Background architecture, plants and people remain smooth, pale suggestions. Dense crowds can stay dense while losing individual faces, garments and anatomy. A focal person or group remains coherent.
- **Connected washes.** Luminous near-white upper passages, broad blue dilutions and restrained warm light. Grounded scenes use a solid, continuous floor wash and natural contact shadow rather than scattered reflections or repeated stripes. Preserve selected green in Madinah and Rawdah.
- **Natural surface.** Fine paper grain and wet pigment transitions. Avoid repeated cellular or floral patterns, wrinkles, glossy CGI and decorative wisps. Accepted reference texture is not permission to amplify it in new work.

### Colour discipline

Match the actual reference blue across dark cores, midtones and pale washes; never invent a single brand hex. Keep gold soft and whites neutral. Avoid electric blue, cyan drift and neutral black as substitutes for the painted blue.

A blue-tone-only correction changes hue/chroma while preserving light/dark values, white balance, composition and detail. Preserve an accepted palette through unrelated edits. Use the colour procedure for a requested numerical blue match; painting, spatial changes and value edits require the image tool.

### Creation and refinement

Use built-in Imagegen for raster painting. Assign references clear roles: painting/palette, exact edit target and necessary subject geometry. For a continuation, preserve the selected crop, pose, scale and layout unless a change is requested. Reconstructing from scratch changes the painting rather than the agreed composition.

Keep bounded edits local. In a colour-only step, do not darken shadows; in a position-only step, move the subject and its grounding shadow without redesigning the scene. A blank folded map remains unmarked unless information is requested.

Inspect the actual output for reference resemblance, one focus, economical construction, intact geometry, soft supporting forms, natural lost edges and correct colour. Refine observed mismatches, normally within two focused passes. Report remaining limitations accurately. Save the final artwork separately with its prompt and concise QA record; add new references only when requested.

## Iconography

Simple forms on a shared grid (*icon-structure*): consistent strokes; rounded caps, terminals and joins, keeping the corners that define the subject; gaps open enough to read small; scale and position balanced by eye. Pick icons that clearly name the service, action or information, and use them consistently in size, colour and alignment (*icon-library-1*, *icon-library-2*). Three treatments, outline, two-tone and filled (*icon-library-treatments*): pick one per context and never alter the construction. Product screens use the icons published in the DL Icons file.

## Art direction (new in this edition)

For brand layouts: posters, banners, social posts, store graphics, decks and campaign screens. Product screens keep the DL spacing.

- **Nusuk margin (*art-margin*, *art-layouts*):** 5% of the layout's longest side, the same on every edge. Text, logos, calls to action and essential information stay in the content area; nothing goes in the outer margin. A 1080 × 1920 story has a 96 px margin; a 1440 × 240 leaderboard, 72 px.
- **Primary graphic device, the Nusuk Frame (*art-nusuk-frame*, *art-nusuk-frame-use*):** derived from the outer shape of the brandmark, for general Nusuk communication. Use the whole frame as a standalone shape, extend it beyond the layout edges, scale and crop it for emphasis, or use a cropped section as a container: a solid backdrop for text over imagery, a subtle photographic treatment, a container for imagery, a divider between image and text.
- **Supporting device #1, the Makkah Frame (*art-makkah-frame*, *art-makkah-frame-use*):** from the outer form of the Kaaba, geometric, for Makkah-specific communication (the city, its sacred destinations, pilgrimage). It may repeat to build controlled patterns.
- **Supporting device #2, the Madinah Frame (*art-madinah-frame*, *art-madinah-frame-use*):** from the outer form of the Green Dome, curved, for Madinah-specific communication (the city, its sacred destinations, visitation).
- **Rules (*art-rules*):** the primary device for general communication, a supporting device for its destination; keep each device's proportions and contours when scaling, cropping or repeating; one dominant element; the 5% margin; the defined colour roles with clear contrast; the photography and illustration rules (soft grain on every illustration).
- **Social feed rhythm (*art-social-feed*):** groups of three posts in the order Gold → White → Black → White → Green → White, then repeat. White is the pause between colours. Vary photography, illustration and graphics inside a group while its dominant colour holds; adjoining posts may form one composition, with text clear of the divisions.
