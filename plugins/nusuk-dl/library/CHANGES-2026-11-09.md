# Nusuk DL · 9 November 2026 · Button sizes, motion, chips and the review, for iOS and Android

For developers and coding agents. Figma is the source of truth: ⚙️ Foundation › Layout › `Buttons/*` and 🍟 Chips › `_Chips Setup`, read 9 November 2026. Tokens 3.2. Full detail: DESIGN.md › M9 (Buttons, Toolbar, Chip) and M11.

## Rules for agents

1. Change heights only. Padding, gaps, icon sizes, colours and states are as on 8 November.
2. Read heights from the tokens, never hard-code them: `button-height-lr | md | sm | xs`.
3. Keep the 48 target: MD, SM and XS CTAs get invisible padding; top-bar controls stay 44.

## Motion (evening)

The Nusuk AI shimmer in the home tab bar plays once (3 s) when the bar appears, then rests; replay it only for a new reason (a new suggestion). Rule for every platform (M8, M10): no resting loops; motion that starts by itself stops within 5 s (WCAG 2.2.2). Spinners and typing dots loop only while the wait lasts; all motion stops under Reduce Motion (iOS) and Remove animations (Android).

## Review (late night): targets, names, focus

Nothing changes in size, colour or layout. Three things to build, all measured in the coded components:

1. **Tap padding.** Every control answers a 48 touch (44 in the top bar) whatever its drawn size. Use `contentShape` (iOS) and `minimumInteractiveComponentSize()` or `Modifier.minimumInteractiveComponentSize` (Android); never grow the drawn control. Amounts per control: DESIGN.md › M11 › Tap padding (CTA MD +2, SM +5, XS +9 above and below; checkbox +14, radio +15 all round; switch +8 sides and +15.5 above and below; stepper buttons +14; more +12; top-bar icon buttons +3 to 44). Keep 48 between the centres of stacked selection controls.
2. **Names.** Every control has a name in the current language. A visible label is the field's name and the hint its description; a placeholder is never the only name. Icon buttons name the outcome ("رجوع", "إغلاق", "السلة"), never the icon. The coded components hold the Arabic and English defaults in `NusukDL.strings`.
3. **Focus ring.** Ink, 2 px, offset 2, for keyboard, switch control and D-pad; white on Ink fills and dark glass. Not gold.

Also: on a fill that inverts in dark themes (Ink), text and icons use a colour that inverts (`surface-01-white`), not `text-inverse`. The Member Card's swipe actions sit on the trailing side in both directions.
